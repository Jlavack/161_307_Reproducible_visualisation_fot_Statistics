      Kia ora John, and welcome to the course.

      The course is new for 2026 and we have put a lot of time and effort into developing the content.

      Our goals are to teach you the skills and tools that statisticians and data scientists use daily for summarising, displaying, and analysing data to tell stories and understand our world.

      We'll be diving deep into the world of ggplot2 in particular to create engaging visualisations, using code for all steps of the process.

      We hope you enjoy the topic as much as we do, and we are keen to get feedback from you on things you like as well as on things you don’t like.

        Student led discussion groups

        The purpose of these discussion groups (via zoom) is for you to discuss aspects of data visualisation with your peers.

        It is expected that you will meet with your group fortnightly for a short (20-30 min) discussion of a small piece of work you have completed (e.g. find a visualisation or piece of data journalism you like, or read a short paper related to some aspect of visualisation) and that one person from each group will write a short summary of the discussion on the stream forum.
